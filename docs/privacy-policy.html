Shadowman Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

In short: Shadowman does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your game progress is stored only on your own device.

Who we are

Shadowman (package name com.shadowman) is a single-player offline platform game developed by KinngShishupal ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for, access or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details
  • Your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage analytics

Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves a small amount of game data locally on your device:

  • Chapters unlocked, marks earned and best times
  • Your music and sound on/off settings

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Network access

Shadowman plays fully offline and does not send any data over the internet.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services, and we do not share any data with third parties. If you download the app from Google Play, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy; we do not receive that information.

Children's privacy

Shadowman is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
